## 👤 Who I Am

Senior Fullstack Engineer with 5+ years specializing in **React, TypeScript, and Applied AI**. My work sits at the intersection of high-performance frontend engineering and intelligent systems — from Canvas-based 3D visualizations to Agentic RAG workflows that let developers query complex legacy codebases through natural language.

I believe good software is engineered the same way a great car is designed: every component earns its place, failure is anticipated and prevented (*poka-yoke*), and the whole system serves the person using it — not the other way around.

## ⚙️ Engineering Philosophy

<table>
<tr>
<td width="50%">

### 🔄 Kaizen — Continuous Improvement

Toyota's principle of continuous, incremental improvement is how I approach codebases. Every PR is an opportunity to leave things measurably better.

At my current role this meant leading a **PHP → React migration with zero downtime**, establishing TypeScript-first standards that cut runtime errors by ~30%, and shipping internal AI tooling that compounds developer velocity over time.

</td>
<td width="50%">

### 🏎️ Jinba-ittai — Rider and Horse as One

Mazda's design ideal of perfect unity between driver and machine. The best developer tools disappear — the engineer stops fighting the stack and starts thinking only about the problem.

It's why I obsess over DX: clean APIs, predictable abstractions, and AI systems that augment intent rather than introduce friction.

</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="50%">

### 🔩 Poka-yoke — Mistake-Proofing

Design systems so errors are impossible, not just unlikely. TypeScript strict mode, automated security scanning in CI, linting rules that enforce contracts — prevention over detection.

</td>
<td width="50%">

### 🏭 Monozukuri — The Art of Making

Craft matters. Architecture documents, typed interfaces, readable code, and clear commit history are all part of the product. The code is the artifact.

</td>
</tr>
</table>
